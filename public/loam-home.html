anxiety isn’t who you are. it’s who you aren’t.

Loam is built on two hypotheses

  1. i.
    Anxiety fills the space we give it.
    And we can give it less.
  2. ii.
    Our realities are defined by the stories we tell ourselves.
    And we can edit them.
