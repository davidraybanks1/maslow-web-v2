What Loam is built on

two hypotheses.

Anxiety fills the space we give it.
And we can give it less.
Our realities are defined by the stories we tell ourselves.
And we can edit them.
needs

live beyond survival-mode

Prioritize your needs based on your unique experience with anxiety, goals, and season of life. Some needs will be in survival-mode, but that’s not the default.

Modes
vibrations

be honest with yourself

Three times a day, press a button to document how you feel. Your vibrations provide a barometer to measure how well everything you are working on is working for you.

discover what makes for good days

The information from your modes, practices, and vibrations turns into data that helps pinpoint what to add, subtract, or tweak.

drafts

talk about yourself to yourself.

Write as often as you like — a line at breakfast, a paragraph at midnight. Tag drafts with how they felt and which need they touched, and this becomes the color commentary for the trends in your data.
